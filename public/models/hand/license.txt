Model information:
* What:    the right hand and lower forearm of a human made with MPFB 2 (MakeHuman Plugin For Blender)
* Source:  https://static.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb.html
* Assets:  MPFB 2 base mesh and "game_engine" skeleton, and the MakeHuman system assets skin
           "young_asian_male" (makehuman_system_assets_cc0.zip)

Changes made for SongKhem:
* kept the hand and 12 cm of forearm, smoothed one level (13,744 triangles), given its own UV layout
* new skin colour and detail maps baked in Blender (knuckle and fingertip colour, folds, veins,
  fine hair, creases, fingerprints)
* posed round a phone the size of the one on the site; three thumb poses (away, hover, press) are
  stored as the animation "grip", and the node "PhoneAnchor" marks where the phone sits in the hand
